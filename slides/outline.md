# Presentation Outline — Customer Churn Insights

A slide-by-slide narrative for presenting the churn analysis to non-technical
stakeholders. Pairs with Section 5 ("Presentation Strategy") of the full report.

1. **Title & framing** — "Why are we losing customers, and what can we do about it?"
2. **Headline scorecard** — Figure 6 (KPI scorecard). Lead with the answer before the analysis.
3. **The trend** — Figure 1 (churn rate over time), tied to the retention program launch.
4. **Why it's happening** — Figure 2 (top churn drivers), translated into plain language.
5. **Who it's happening to** — Figure 3 (segment comparison, before vs. after).
6. **How confident are we** — Figure 4 (ROC curve), described as "correctly flags ~4 in 5 churners."
7. **The opportunity hiding in the data** — Figure 5 (anomaly cluster), framed as a high-ROI action item.
8. **Recommendations & the ask** — immediate next steps, with a specific decision requested.
9. **Appendix (only if asked)** — methodology detail, model metrics, data sources.

**Delivery notes:** one idea per slide, consistent color coding (red = risk, blue =
performance), dollar-denominated impact wherever possible, and a reserved 5–10 minutes
for discussion at the end.
